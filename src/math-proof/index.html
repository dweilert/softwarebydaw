What the checks mean

  • Authority-validated: the expected result comes from an IRS, CMS, or Social Security publication, worksheet, or worked example.
  • Cross-validated: the same published methodology was independently reimplemented and compared.
  • Vetted-secondary: used only when a primary source is unavailable, with the limitation disclosed.
  • Regression-only: an internal consistency check, not a claim of outside authority.

What this does not mean

Passing calculation cases do not make RP a licensed financial advisor, guarantee an outcome, or prove that a plan is optimal. The report identifies known limitations and the areas where assumptions, projections, or simplified modeling matter.
