The Developer

A long career in technology, now focused on building practical software and sharing it openly.

Dave Weilert
Software developer and IT leader

Experience behind the software

Dave started his IT career in the mid 1970s — yes, they had computers then. Since then, his work has spanned many parts of information technology, including operations, software development, system design, technical leadership, and sales.

Over that career he held multiple C-level roles and spent more than 30 years working in the financial services and insurance industries, where reliability, auditability, and clear communication matter.

The software on this site reflects that background: practical tools, built to be useful, shared openly, and documented for people who want to understand what the software is doing.
