Software

Open source, free to use. Retirement Planner and VpK are the two actively developed flagship projects — dataloader and datapull are a pair of smaller, focused command-line utilities.

01 — Flagship project

Retirement Planner

"Will the money last?" A free, open-source, and completely private retirement planner with the depth of a paid advisory tool — tax-aware Monte Carlo, Social Security optimization, Roth conversions, IRMAA, and estate planning. No accounts, no subscription, nothing ever leaves your device.

Learn more
02 — Flagship project

VpK

Visually presented Kubernetes (VpK) turns a live cluster's tangle of resources into an interactive diagram — see what's deployed and how it's wired together, without memorizing kubectl output. Open source, built for anyone who has ever lost time hunting through YAML to understand what's actually running.

03 & 04 — Developer utilities

dataloader & datapull

A small paired set of command-line tools for moving data in and out of Apache CouchDB or IBM Cloudant.

dataloader

Loads delimited data files straight into Apache CouchDB or IBM Cloudant. Point it at a file, and your documents are in the database.

View on GitHub
datapull

Pulls data back out of Apache CouchDB or IBM Cloudant and writes it to a delimited file — the companion to dataloader, for extracting or migrating what's already there.

View on GitHub
