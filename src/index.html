Retirement planning without the sales call

You should be able to test retirement decisions without connecting every account, paying a yearly fee, or waiting for someone else to run the numbers.

Retirement Planner lets you build the plan yourself: download it, enter what matters, save it locally, and compare choices on your own schedule.

It is built for people making real household decisions: retiring sooner, claiming Social Security, converting to Roth, changing spending, preparing for a survivor, or seeing whether taxes and Medicare premiums change the answer.

The goal is not to bury you in every possible knob. It is to show the few decisions that move the plan, with enough detail available when you want to look under the hood.

The questions it helps answer

Competitors often lead with feature names. Retirement Planner is organized around the decisions a household actually has to make.

Can I retire earlier?

Try different retirement dates, income gaps, market crashes, inflation decades, and longer lifespans in the same plan.

How much can I spend?

Compare fixed budgets, flexible spending guardrails, and a living-standard estimate that helps turn savings into a spending plan.

When should we claim Social Security?

Run household claim-age comparisons that price the savings spent while waiting for a larger benefit.

Should I convert to Roth?

Compare conversion years, annual limits, tax-bracket targets, Medicare surcharge effects, and survivor impact.

Will taxes or IRMAA change the answer?

Include federal, state, local, capital gains, Social Security taxation, RMDs, ACA credits, and Medicare Part B/D surcharges.

What happens to the survivor?

Test first-death scenarios, survivor filing status, income floor changes, insurance needs, and estate outcomes.

What it does

A working list, not a wish list — every item below is real and shipping today.

See the actual calculation

The Engine Viewer shows the real intermediate values behind every number — RMD divisors, tax-bracket math, IRMAA tier assignment. Not a black box.

Social Security timing

Compares claim-age choices for a household, including spouse and survivor effects, so you can see which timing looks strongest.

Roth conversion planner

Preview tax-bracket and Medicare surcharge impact before you commit to a Roth conversion plan.

Market-risk testing

See how the plan holds up when investment returns arrive in a rougher order than expected.

Taxes & Medicare costs

Federal, state, and county/city income tax, capital gains, and Medicare IRMAA surcharge projection with the real two-year lookback.

Estate & legacy projection

Trusts, lifetime gifting, heir bequest splits, and inherited-account rules (10-year rule, EDB stretch) — plus an At-Death Checklist built from your own plan.

Historical stress tests

Replay the plan through real market histories to see how earlier retirements would have handled rough periods.

Built-in guided training

Guided lessons walk you through the app screen by screen so you can learn by working with your own plan.

Local-first & private

No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry. Your plan never leaves your machine unless you export it yourself.

Why trust the math

A retirement plan is easier to trust when it shows what was included, what was checked, and what was left out. I have not found another consumer retirement planner that publicly exposes its calculation-maintenance process and optimizer checks as directly as Retirement Planner does — other tools may do similar work internally, but their public materials do not show it.

Validation reports

Source citations

Known limits

The app documents approximations and omissions up front, including which direction a simplification tends to bias the result.

Where Retirement Planner stands

A private retirement planning system with deep tax, Medicare, Social Security, Roth, withdrawal, survivor, estate, and market-risk tools. The personal edition is built for households that want control of their own plan. The Agency edition adds the book-of-business workflow a practice needs to manage client plans, reviews, snapshots, retention opportunities, and Pro-Active daily monitoring.

Why RP is different

  • Installable app: macOS, Windows, and Linux delivery path exists.
  • Local-first: plan files stay on the user's device.
  • Embedded training: narrated courses drive the real product, open the actual screens, and point at the actual controls.
  • Supplemental courses: new courses and course series can be created, shared, installed, and updated after the app is already deployed.
  • Agency edition: client catalog, branded reports, snapshots, work queue, retention tools, custom training, and daily monitoring workflow.
  • Calculation maintenance: the tax, Medicare, and Social Security numbers are reviewed, checked, and re-tested before updates go out.
Agency edition changes the comparison

RP is no longer just a household planning app.

Agency adds the practice layer that moves RP closer to Income Lab: a client catalog over local/shared plan folders, private advisor notes and review dates, branded client reports, automatic presentation snapshots, a Practice Worklist that becomes the queue, daily account sweeps, monitoring tolerances, outreach templates, FlowThru retention opportunities, example clients for training, and a Course Editor so a firm can teach its own process inside the product and share supplemental courses with others.

Closer to Income Lab Income Lab still has mature integrations and advisor workflow depth. RP Agency now has enough practice infrastructure to be compared in the advisor lane, and RP's sweep cadence can be daily rather than monthly.

Client Catalog

Point RP at a client folder and work from the client first: plans, private notes, review dates, snapshots, and documents stay organized without turning RP into a cloud CRM.

Presentation Record

Agency snapshots preserve what the client was shown, including the plan file, reports, and reference-data vintage. That creates a defensible meeting record.

Practice Worklist

The worklist turns monitoring findings into advisor work: acknowledge, schedule, mark done, snooze, and add private notes without changing the client plan.

Pro-Active Monitoring

RP’s scheduled sweeps already check client impact across the book of business: plan drift, stale assumptions, review cadence, estate/FlowThru successor-plan changes, and client-level tolerances.

Flexible Sweep Cadence

Income Lab is commonly described as a monthly monitoring loop. RP can run daily, or use the frequency the agency wants, with client-level settings where a household needs different attention.

Retention via FlowThru

FlowThru can turn estate planning into a next-generation relationship: each heir can receive a linked successor plan, giving the agency a natural reason to keep the family engaged.

Custom Training

Agencies can use Course Editor and Course Series to build their own guided training, mix it with RP’s built-in courses, and teach the firm’s planning process in the same app clients and advisors use.

Shared Course Packs

Supplemental courses and series can be distributed after install, so an agency, trainer, or product team can publish new walkthroughs without waiting for a full app release.

Standout RP feature

FlowThru linked estate plans

Retirement Planner can turn an estate plan into linked successor plans for heirs, so the conversation does not stop at “what is left?” It can continue into what that inheritance means for each person receiving it. For an agency, that also creates a real retention bridge from one generation to the next.

Standout RP feature

Affordable Spending from two lenses

RP combines consumption smoothing with guardrails: one side solves a sustainable living standard, the other tests how spending can adapt. Together they produce an Affordable Spending view instead of a single brittle number.

What-If stress lab

Market history, Monte Carlo, longevity, early retirement, income shock, high inflation, Social Security cuts, survivor IRMAA, relocation, and affordability checks.

Decision tools

Roth, withdrawal order, claim age, contributions, retirement timing, pension vs. lump sum, debt vs. invest, insurance surrender, and asset sales.

Joint optimizers

RP does not only test one knob at a time: it can cross Social Security with Roth conversions, and Roth conversions with withdrawal order.

Household risk

Survivor protection, lifetime income floor, long-term care exposure, estate flow, and at-death actions are connected to the actual plan.

Training built into the product

Courses are not separate videos. They open RP, highlight the live control, wait for the user, and can be extended by agencies with their own custom or supplemental courses and series.
