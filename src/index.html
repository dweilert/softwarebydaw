Retirement planning without the sales call

You should be able to test retirement decisions without connecting every account, paying a yearly fee, or waiting for someone else to run the numbers.

Retirement Planner lets you build the plan yourself: download it, enter what matters, save it locally, and compare choices on your own schedule.

It is built for people making real household decisions: retiring sooner, claiming Social Security, converting to Roth, changing spending, preparing for a survivor, or seeing whether taxes and Medicare premiums change the answer.

The goal is not to bury you in every possible knob. It is to show the few decisions that move the plan, with enough detail available when you want to look under the hood.

The questions it helps answer

Competitors often lead with feature names. Retirement Planner is organized around the decisions a household actually has to make.

Can I retire earlier?

Try different retirement dates, income gaps, market crashes, inflation decades, and longer lifespans in the same plan.

How much can I spend?

Compare fixed budgets, flexible spending guardrails, and a living-standard estimate that helps turn savings into a spending plan.

When should we claim Social Security?

Run household claim-age comparisons that price the savings spent while waiting for a larger benefit.

Should I convert to Roth?

Compare conversion years, annual limits, tax-bracket targets, Medicare surcharge effects, and survivor impact.

Will taxes or IRMAA change the answer?

Include federal, state, local, capital gains, Social Security taxation, RMDs, ACA credits, and Medicare Part B/D surcharges.

What happens to the survivor?

Test first-death scenarios, survivor filing status, income floor changes, insurance needs, and estate outcomes.

What it does

A working list, not a wish list — every item below is real and shipping today.

See the actual calculation

The Engine Viewer shows the real intermediate values behind every number — RMD divisors, tax-bracket math, IRMAA tier assignment. Not a black box.

Social Security timing

Compares claim-age choices for a household, including spouse and survivor effects, so you can see which timing looks strongest.

Roth conversion planner

Preview tax-bracket and Medicare surcharge impact before you commit to a Roth conversion plan.

Market-risk testing

See how the plan holds up when investment returns arrive in a rougher order than expected.

Taxes & Medicare costs

Federal, state, and county/city income tax, capital gains, and Medicare IRMAA surcharge projection with the real two-year lookback.

Estate & legacy projection

Trusts, lifetime gifting, heir bequest splits, and inherited-account rules (10-year rule, EDB stretch) — plus an At-Death Checklist built from your own plan.

Historical stress tests

Replay the plan through real market histories to see how earlier retirements would have handled rough periods.

Built-in guided training

Guided lessons walk you through the app screen by screen so you can learn by working with your own plan.

Local-first & private

No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry. Your plan never leaves your machine unless you export it yourself.

Why trust the math

A retirement plan is easier to trust when it shows what was included, what was checked, and what was left out.

Validation reports

Every release includes a public report comparing tax, Medicare, Social Security, RMD, and estate calculations against source documents and test output.

Source citations

Tax, Medicare, and Social Security data includes source notes and freshness checks so you can see where the numbers came from.

Known limits

The app documents approximations and omissions up front, including which direction a simplification tends to bias the result.

Open source

The source code, tests, data tables, and release files are public rather than hidden behind a service account.

Said plainly: what you're trading away

No product is best at everything, and pretending otherwise wouldn't be honest:

  • No bank/brokerage account syncing — this is a private, offline tool by design.
  • No mobile app — it runs as a desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux, or directly in the browser.
  • No human coaching or advisor access, unlike Boldin's paid tier.
  • No automatic brokerage data feed, unlike account-aggregation-first services.
  • ProjectionLab's quick visual scenario workflow is still a smoother fit for lightweight exploration.
  • MaxiFi and Pralana have long-standing reputations among detailed retirement-planning users.
