Cash flow & budget (18)

Budget

Your spending, line by line — the core of “will the money last?”. Any line can also be flagged as originally in a foreign currency (pick from 20 major currencies, enter in either currency, check today's rate or sync to the plan default), informational only.

Currency Exchange tool

A standalone converter (Tools nav group): pick a currency, check today's USD rate, and optionally save it as this plan's default for that currency. Income/Budget foreign-currency lines can sync to a saved default with one click instead of checking the rate again themselves, and get a warning if their own checked rate ever drifts from it. Purely a convenience — never feeds the projection, which always runs in USD.

Retirement Spending Shape (go-go/slow-go/no-go)

A customizable, editable-point curve that scales Optional-tagged budget lines by years-into-retirement — default preset is +15% early, tapering to −15% by year 20, but every point (year + percentage) can be added, edited, or removed — modeling the classic pattern of front-loaded discretionary spending that eases off later. Independent of the active spending policy. Required lines and home/rental ongoing-cost lines aren't affected.

Housing

Own or rent — pick one to start. Owning models up to 2 homes, each with its own mortgage, optional HELOC, optional reverse mortgage, ongoing costs, cost basis, and planned sale; renting tracks monthly rent (its own inflation rate), lease details, and renter expenses. Switch between them with the pill selector at the top of the screen.

Vehicles

Vehicle values, loans, and operating expenses. A scheduled sell year converts that vehicle's depreciated value, net of any remaining loan payoff, into cash the following year.

Payment Types

The cards and bank accounts you pay from — the “pay with” on every spending line.

Credit card interest-rate warning

Enter a card's current APR, and — if it's a promotional/intro rate — the date it ends and the rate afterward. Plan Health flags a card already at your high-interest threshold (Settings → Plan model), or one whose promo rate ends within 90 days and jumps above it. A rate-only check — this app doesn't track card balances.

Required / Optional tags

Tag each spending line as essential or discretionary — the split the whole app turns on.

Spending policy

Fixed budget, the 4% rule, VPW, guardrails — or an adopted living standard.

Protect essential spending

In a tight year, trim discretionary instead of draining the portfolio.

Home dashboard

The at-a-glance verdict: money-lasts pill, essentials pill, headline metrics, a what's-configured checklist, and up to 13 chart chips (which ones show is configurable from Settings).

Essentials coverage

Whether the basics stay funded even if savings run out — reads your Required tags.

Cash Flow Sankey

One year's money flow at a glance — every source in, every category out, debt payments grouped.

Debt payment grouping

Living Standard (consumption smoothing)

Solve what you could spend, level, for life — then optionally adopt or rescale to it.

Shared-living factor

Taxes & IRMAA (12)

Taxes

Filing status, state, and the tax settings the engine computes against.

Withdrawal order

Which tax families drain first (Taxable → Tax-deferred → Roth → HSA → Annuity) — one of the biggest tax-efficiency levers you control. When you hold 2+ accounts of the same family, the engine now also drains them in the order they're listed on the Assets screen (reorder arrows on taxable accounts), rather than splitting proportionally — so a taxable account's own embedded gain, not a blended average, determines the capital gain realized when it's drawn.

Account tax detail (basis & inherited rules)

Per-account fields that make the tax exact: annuity investment-in-contract (LIFO), nondeductible IRA basis (Form 8606 pro-rata), and inherited-account beneficiary rules (10-year / EDB stretch, Roth, drawdown strategy). Early (pre-59½) withdrawals also carry the 10% penalty automatically — with two real exceptions modeled per-account: Rule of 55 (separating from an employer plan at 55+) and 72(t)/SEPP (a fixed annual withdrawal schedule). A 401(k)/403(b)/TSP account can also model an NUA (Net Unrealized Appreciation) election — a one-time in-kind distribution of the whole account to a taxable account, taxing only your cost basis now and the rest at LTCG rates on eventual sale.

Income-linked contributions (401(k)-style saving)

Set a pre-retirement contribution as a percent of a named earned-income stream instead of a flat dollar amount — it scales with the salary. A pre-tax deferral lowers that year's taxable income; the amount above the IRS 402(g) limit spills into a named overflow account (routing it to a Roth account models a mega backdoor Roth, capped at the real §415(c) overall limit); and the employer match can be a two-tier fraction-of-pay formula. Plan Health flags an uncovered overflow or an unclaimed match.

Projection table

The year-by-year detail: budget, income, withdrawals, RMDs, taxes, IRMAA, balances.

Required Minimum Distributions

Forced withdrawals from tax-deferred accounts at age — computed automatically.

IRMAA tier projection

Medicare surcharges when income crosses a threshold — with the two-year lag.

Survivor IRMAA Stress Test

The tax & Medicare cliff a survivor faces — and how Roth conversions soften it.

Retirement Timing Scanner

Sweep candidate retirement years (or, for a couple, a 2D grid of each spouse's own year) and compare balance, taxes, and IRMAA.

Roth conversion analysis

Defuse the RMD tax bomb: strategies, an optimizer, and a year-by-year plan.

Tax-loss harvesting

A flat planning assumption (off by default), not a simulated per-lot mechanism: shelters an assumed fraction of each year's realized long-term capital gains, plus optionally the flat $3,000/yr ordinary-income offset IRS Code §1211(b) allows.

Gains harvesting

Off by default. The mirror of tax-loss harvesting, but a real computed mechanism: each year realizes just enough long-term capital gain via a same-day sell-and-rebuy to fill headroom in the federal 0% LTCG bracket, permanently stepping up taxable-account cost basis tax-free.

Investments & withdrawals (10)

Assets & accounts

Investment and cash accounts. Pick from the full set of tax types — Traditional/Roth IRA, 401(k)/Roth 401(k), TSP, 403(b), 457(b), SEP/SIMPLE, HSA, annuity, taxable — grouped by tax family, each with a return assumption.

Emergency Fund tracker

A target expressed in months of essential (Required-priority) budget spending, with a progress bar against your cash accounts. Shares its target with Plan Health's cash-reserve check (G29), so changing it in either place updates both.

Equity compensation (RSUs, NSOs, ISOs & ESPPs)

RSU, NSO, ISO, and ESPP grants — full FMV taxed for an RSU, the FMV-minus-strike spread for an NSO, a qualifying/disqualifying disposition split for an ISO at a planned sale year, and the same kind of split (its own formula) for an ESPP purchase at a planned sale year. No AMT computed for ISOs — Plan Health flags large exercises instead.

Funded ratio

Assets today vs. the present value of what you'll need — a stock-based second opinion.

What-If levers

Preview the seven biggest dials against your baseline without committing.

Monte Carlo

Randomize returns to surface sequence-of-returns risk; Accurate mode is tax-aware. Volatility can be a manual dial or derived from your accounts' own allocations.

Historical backtest

Replay your plan through every real market era from 1928 on.

Annuity Purchase Comparison

Compares keeping money invested against moving a hypothetical amount into a new annuity account instead — the engine's own nonqualified-annuity LIFO taxation applies to it. Lifetime tax, terminal balance, and whether money lasts the horizon, side by side. Doesn't model an insurer's guaranteed payout or mortality credits. Ephemeral — doesn't change the saved plan.

Insurance Cash-Value Surrender Comparison

Compares keeping a cash-value insurance policy against surrendering it and investing the after-tax net proceeds instead. Computes real ordinary-income tax on the gain above your entered cost basis (FIFO cost-recovery), plus a user-entered surrender charge. A prominent, non-computed warning stays on screen: surrendering permanently forfeits the death benefit — the actual trade-off, not a footnote. Ephemeral — doesn't change the saved plan.

Sell It All

A single, sortable table listing every liquidatable asset in the plan — homes, rental properties, owned (non-leased) vehicles, and cash-value insurance policies — with current value and any planned sale year, each linking straight to that asset's own real sale-setup screen. Read-only aggregation only; it doesn't simulate selling multiple assets together (no order-of-sale, reinvest-vs-spend, or tax-bunching modeling — see WISHLIST.md).

Income & Social Security (7)

Income streams

Pensions, annuities, Social Security, and other recurring or one-time income — with presets for Railroad Retirement (Tier I/II) and tax-free VA benefits, an optional Royalty / Business income report category, a Phased retirement quick-entry that splits a stream into a full-time + reduced phase-down pair, a Pause income quick-entry for a job loss or disability gap (with optional resume year), and an optional foreign-currency flag (pick from 20 major currencies, enter in either currency with a direction toggle, a user-invoked "Check today's rate" lookup, and an optional plan-wide default rate from the Currency Exchange tool) for a stream originally paid in another currency.

Rental property

Rental income, expenses, depreciation, and sale — modeled on Schedule E.

Survivor scenario setup

Configure who dies first and when — the input the survivor analyses read.

Trust-fund shortfall

Model the projected ~2034 Social Security benefit cut.

SS claim-age optimizer

Find the claim age (or couple's pair) that maximizes lifetime Social Security.

Spousal, survivor & divorced-spouse Social Security

The 50% spousal benefit and the survivor keeping the higher of two records for a couple; a divorced-spouse benefit (up to 50% of an ex's PIA, no requirement the ex has claimed) for a single person too.

Lump Sum vs. Pension

Compares keeping a pension/annuity income stream against taking a hypothetical lump sum and rolling it into a Traditional/Roth IRA (or similar) account instead — lifetime tax, terminal balance, and whether money lasts the horizon, side by side. Models a rollover only, not a taxable cash-out. Ephemeral — doesn't change the saved plan.

Medical & insurance

Medicare premiums, pre-Medicare coverage (ACA/COBRA), and long-term-care scenarios.

Long-term care shock

Estate & legacy (6)

Bequest goal

How much you intend to leave heirs — tracked against your projected estate.

Lifetime gifts

Giving money or stock away while alive, distinct from a bequest at death. Debits a taxable account each active year; cash mode realizes capital gains to you, gifting shares directly realizes none (the recipient inherits your cost basis instead of a step-up). Real gift-tax computation isn't modeled — Plan Health flags a recipient's total above the $19,000/yr annual exclusion as a heads-up. Can be tagged to a real Heir instead of free text, or as funding an Irrevocable Trust.

Heirs & Trusts

Named beneficiaries (people or charities) and the trusts that can hold assets for them — the first real "who inherits what" model, beyond a single flat Bequest goal total. A Revocable Living Trust is a pure tag on a home/rental/investment/policy/vehicle/equity-grant/cash-account (zero engine effect — taxed exactly like owning it directly). An Irrevocable Trust is funded via a tagged Lifetime Gift, reusing that feature's real account-draining mechanism as the removal-from-estate behavior. Any asset can also be split among heirs by percentage directly, without a trust — visualized as a Sankey diagram (chart button + Brochure report) showing exactly which source flows to which heir.

FlowThru — a linked succession plan per heir

Expanding a heir's row on the Estate screen and clicking "Create FlowThru plan…" builds a linked, separate plan file ("{plan}.flowthru.{heir name}.rplan.json") seeded from everything currently assigned to that heir (direct allocations, trust beneficiary shares, irrevocable-trust funded value), at a target year chosen from this plan's own projection — the source plan is never modified. Multi-heir: any number of heirs can each have their own active FlowThru simultaneously, fully independent of each other. Homes/vehicles allocated to the heir can flow through, be replaced with an estimated cost, or be marked as sold (net proceeds as a cash lump sum); medical and all-other annual costs can be estimated too, seeded as payment-method-free budget lines. A tax-deferred source becomes an inherited account in the new plan, reusing the existing InheritedInfo 10-year-rule/RMD machinery; a home, rental, policy, or irrevocable-trust source becomes a plain taxable account. Open any heir's FlowThru with one click and return via a floating, draggable pill + red glow, with a startup warning if a heir's FlowThru file goes missing (Phase 0/1/1.5/2 all built, DESIGN_flowthru.md). Equity grants and cash accounts are assignable to heirs/trusts (see Heirs & Trusts) but not yet copied into a FlowThru plan — that's separate, unbuilt scope.

Estate snapshot

Terminal balance, step-up basis, inherited-IRA rules, bequest tracking, held-in-trust reporting, and federal + state estate-tax exemption warnings (12 states + DC).

At-Death Checklist

What a death triggers: per-person actions DERIVED from your plan (claim survivor SS, roll over/inherit each account, step-up basis, file life claims), plus the real-world administrative & legal to-dos done outside the app — grouped by timing, and flagging money that must be RETURNED (SS/pensions don't pay for the month of death). Track plan-derived items as Notes tied to that person. Not an engine input.

Workflow & data (15)

Individuals

The people in the plan — birth dates, retirement ages (a household year, or each spouse's own), the household itself.

Data Explorer

A Sankey view of every plan category — sections on the left (an individual, a home, a budget group, …), that section's fields on the right, colored green/amber/gray for entered / flagged / missing.

Scenario Check

Pick a common retirement question ("Can I afford to retire now?", "What if I die first?", …) and see whether enough is entered to answer it — data-readiness checklist, a Monte Carlo-derived odds-of-success figure, and a plain-language difficulty rating, all shown on screen, plus a written PDF answer with heavy disclaimer language. Not the same feature as Scenario branching (below) — this checks one question against your current plan, it doesn't create alternate plan files.

Quick Start (Rapid Ready)

Settings → App → Reset & Recovery → "⚡ Quick Start — build my own plan…". A ~10-question one-modal form (name, birth date, marital status, state, retirement year, income, total savings, monthly spending, Social Security estimate) that builds a real plan from your own numbers in about two minutes, instead of entering data screen by screen. Deliberately rough: the lump-sum savings answer is split across taxable/traditional/401(k)/Roth using the same ratio the app's own default plan uses; no housing, vehicles, rental, or itemized budget. Discards the current plan on submit (same as Reset to example plan); Home shows a dismissible banner afterward with a "what to refine first" punch list. For someone who wants a real number fast and would rather refine later than fill out every screen first — distinct from Built-in Training (teaches you the screens) and the 5 example plans (someone else's numbers).

Built-in Training

"🎓 Start Training" in the sidebar opens a picker — searchable, group-by-Category-or-Status, with a per-lesson status badge (in progress / completed, tracked in localStorage) — then a step-by-step guide in a second window that drives the main window through a lesson: navigates to the right screen, highlights the exact field, and waits for you before advancing. Pick a disposable sample plan to practice on, or your real plan. General lessons span first-time setup, everyday data entry (adding income/budget/assets/payment types), cross-screen life-event workflows (adding a spouse, downsizing, a full estate-plan setup, comparing two branched scenarios), and correction paths (deleting something without leaving orphaned references); a few more "How To" walkthroughs launch directly from a Scenario Check question (age-based glidepath, Guyton-Klinger guardrails) when the real answer is a sequence of clicks rather than a number, in place of the generic odds-of-success figure. Exact roster: read src/training/lessons/index.ts rather than trusting a count here — it changes often.

Course Editor (add-on)

A separate popup window for authoring your own training courses — deliberately not on the Tools nav; reachable from a low-key link on the Help & Guide → About page. Two ways to edit the same underlying markdown file: a raw Markdown tab, or a Guided tab with per-step title/instruction/route/target fields, image upload, and reorder. A step's completion can be manual (user clicks Next), value-present (a field must be filled in), or timed (auto-advances after a delay you set, still clickable early — never auto-advances past the last step). An "Advanced" section per step adds auto-fill (populates a small, hand-registered set of fields automatically when the step becomes active — a no-op for any other field) and a 3x3 floating-overlay position, for an optional on-screen panel (off by default, toggled with a 🎥 button) that shows the current step's text directly in the main window — built for pairing with a screen-recorder, since the separate guide window doesn't show up in a recording of just the main window. Validate checks the route list and for a lesson-id collision with a built-in lesson; Preview launches the real guide window against the draft (always on the disposable sample plan). Courses are saved as files under the app's data folder, loaded at runtime — genuinely separate from the built-in lessons compiled into the app, so a course here never needs a rebuild. Shows up in the regular "Start Training" picker alongside built-in lessons with a Custom badge.
